Scope: fact-check of `data_sources/07_r_adm.md`, `08_fltmps_nec.md`, `12_training_readiness.md` (plus the matching rows in `00_README.md` section 4) against public, unclassified sources. Labels used: **CONFIRMED**, **WRONG**, **OUTDATED**, **UNVERIFIABLE**. [PH]/[PROP] items are judged only on whether they misrepresent the real format, vocabulary or process.

Method notes (for the report writer):

## Q1. R-ADM: identity, expansion, authority, and whether it is still the afloat system of record for PQS/quals/watchbills in Nov 2026

### Takeaway
The spec's core R-ADM facts are right, and the SFTRM quotes can be upgraded from [S-SNIP] to [S] because the 2018 SFTRM text was read directly. Three fixes are needed. (1) One quote is misattributed: the "qualified or interim qualified" rule cites "reference (b)", not R-ADM. (2) The authority applies only "if installed", with Advanced Skills Management (ASM) as the named alternative. (3) Most important: by 2026 the vendor says **STRMS is being installed on every DDG ("mandate to complete all 75 DDGs in 2026")** and manages PQS, training, watch team replacement plans and watchbills. "R-ADM in use, no named replacement" is therefore outdated as a description of the tool a DDG uses in Nov 2026. No public R-ADM sunset notice was found.

## Q2. PQS watchstation numbering and qualification-status vocabulary

### Takeaway
**The 4-digit watchstation numbers (2200 Lookout, 2300 Helmsman, 2400 QMOW, 2500 BMOW) are not Navy NAVEDTRA numbering.** They come from a local Naval Sea Cadet Corps unit's own qualification book ("NSCS GRAYFOX TWR 825"). Navy NAVEDTRA PQS standards use a 100 (Fundamentals) / 200 (Systems) / 300 (Watchstations) structure with 3-digit section numbers such as 301, 302 and line items such as 301.2.1. The meaning of "301" differs from standard to standard. The status vocabulary (qualified, interim qualified, final qualification, under instruction, requalification) is confirmed. However, the SFTRM makes requalification **event-driven**, not periodic, so the `EXPIRED` / `requal_due` modelling misrepresents the real process for ordinary watchstations.

## Q3. Rating/rate abbreviations: are the rate strings realistic in Nov 2026?

### Takeaway
All ratings listed in the brief (FC, GM, ET, IT, STG, OS, QM, BM, GSM, GSE, EM, IC, EN, MM, DC, HT, LS, CS, YN, PS, HM) still exist. QM, FCA and YN have Navy COOL career roadmaps dated **October 2026**. No 2024-2026 merger of QM into BM, or of YN/PS into an "Administration" rating, was found. Surface IT remains "IT"; "ITS" is the submarine service rating. **The real error is that DDG SPY-radar technicians are FCA (Fire Controlman Aegis), a separate service rating since 2017, not FC.** E-3 strings must also follow the apprenticeship group: engineering and hull ratings are "Fireman" (e.g., GSMFN), not "SN".
